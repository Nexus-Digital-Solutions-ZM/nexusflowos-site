What it does

Four systems your station already runs. One place that actually tracks them.

Nexus Flow OS doesn't replace how your team works — it gives every shift, drop, and delivery a record that adds up.

01
⏱

Shift Tracking

Attendants clock in, record meter and dip readings, and hand over — with a supervisor reviewing every handover before it's approved. No shift closes itself.

02
💰

Cash Reconciliation

Threshold drops mid-shift, a final drawer count at close — matched automatically against expected revenue. Variances are explained, not just flagged.

03
⚖

Inventory & Dip Readings

Tank dips recorded before every shift starts. Stock variance against pump meters is caught at the source, not discovered a week later.

04
📱

Mobile Money

Airtel, MTN, and Zamtel payments recorded against the shift they belong to — netted straight into the day's cash picture, not tracked on the side.
